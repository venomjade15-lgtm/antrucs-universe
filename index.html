THE ALONAIDIAN MYSTERY
Film 01 · Story dossier

Neon Sentinel Knight

A mission below the ocean becomes a struggle over knowledge, loyalty, and the price of power.

The Red Flight Squadron travels from Washington, D.C., toward the Solomon Islands to recover Porcelain, a rare material connected to an ancient underwater site. The mission uncovers mechanical traps and engineering that seem far ahead of their time.

Jayson becomes separated from the others when the cave collapses. Believed lost, he finds a deeper chamber containing ancient writings, a locked box, and The Book of the Alonaidian Cave. He also discovers a separate manual describing how to make weapons.

After emerging from the water, Jayson is welcomed by a family, but his attempt to return to America fails. He ends up inside a cargo container being transported to Russia, where he encounters a man working with both government forces and rebels, as well as three scientists who help him develop a suit using the manual.

The story turns tragic when Monic — who had become Optivoc's weapon or tool because of financial pressure — tries to save Jayson and is killed by Optivoc. The exact details of the factions, company, suit, and final confrontation remain open for the creator to refine.

Secrets beneath the surface

Artifacts, technology & lore.

The AU's mysteries can connect grounded human conflicts to a much larger history. These are the known story elements so far.

The Book of the Alonaidian Cave

An ancient book Jayson discovers in a deeper chamber beneath the underwater site. Its full contents, origin, and importance to the wider universe remain to be revealed.

The Weapon-Making Manual

A separate manual found by Jayson that describes how to make weapons. It becomes part of the process used by three scientists in Russia to help develop a suit.

Porcelain

A rare material that the Red Flight Squadron is sent to recover from the ancient underwater site near the Solomon Islands. Its properties and larger significance are still to be established.

The Ancient Underwater Site

A submerged location containing mechanical traps, unusual engineering, ancient writings, and a hidden chamber. It may hold answers, but its full history has not yet been established.

Experimental Powered Suits

Optivoc independently develops a high-tech suit, and Jayson later receives help from three scientists to develop another suit using the manual. Their designs and capabilities should remain distinct as the story evolves.

Russia: Rebels & Government

Jayson meets a man who works with both sides of a government conflict. The factions, company, and man's loyalties are important open questions for the story bible.

The known sequence

Universe timeline.

This is a story-order outline, not a dated chronology. Specific dates and the deeper history will be added when established.

01 — The mission begins

The Red Flight Squadron is sent from Washington, D.C., toward the Solomon Islands to recover Porcelain.

02 — Beneath the ocean

The team discovers an ancient underwater site filled with mechanical traps and remarkable engineering.

03 — Jayson is lost

A cave collapse separates Jayson from the others. The team believes he has died, while Monic refuses to accept that he is gone.

04 — The Alonaidian discovery

Jayson finds a deeper chamber, ancient writings, a locked box, and The Book of the Alonaidian Cave, along with a weapon-making manual.

05 — A journey to Russia

After emerging from the water and being welcomed by a family, Jayson fails to return to America and ends up in a cargo container bound for Russia.

06 — Suits, betrayal & loss

Three scientists help develop a suit using the manual. The conflict escalates, Optivoc attacks, and Monic dies trying to save Jayson.

07 — Slavion's story

The planned second film introduces Slavion, a hero from space. How his story connects to the first film is still undecided.

The creator's vision

One universe. Many ways to tell it.

The Antrucs Universe is an original story world built to grow over time.

The long-term vision includes films and potentially television, comics, games, and novels — all connected by a consistent canon, memorable characters, and mysteries that reward audiences for following the journey.

This website is designed as the universe's central hub: a place for movie announcements, character dossiers, lore, timelines, trailers, artwork, and future releases.

This is an independent creative project. All story information here is based on details shared by the creator; undeveloped details are marked as in development rather than presented as settled canon.
